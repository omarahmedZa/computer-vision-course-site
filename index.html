Unit 1 · Images as signals

1 lesson
  1. 01 Introduction to Computer Vision Explain what CV is, describe the vision pipeline, and represent images as signals with correct pixel, color, histogram, and convolution operations. 18 min
  2. L1a Convolution & kernelsApply blur, sharpen, and edge kernels and see how correlation/convolution shape the output. 4 pts
  3. L1b Intensity transforms & histogramsExplore brightness, contrast, gamma, and how histograms summarize image tone. 4 pts
  4. Checkpoint 1 — image representation, color spaces, and filtering/convolution.

Unit 2 · Geometry and warping

1 lesson
  1. 02 Geometric Transformations & Image Warping Model 2D deformations with transformation matrices and warp/restore images using forward vs inverse mapping with interpolation. 16 min
  2. L2a Transform matrices & compositionCompose translation, rotation, scale, shear, affine, and homography in homogeneous coordinates. 4 pts
  3. L2b Forward vs inverse warping & interpolationCompare warping directions and nearest vs bilinear interpolation on a grid. 4 pts
  4. Checkpoint 2 — transforms, homogeneous coordinates, warping, and resampling.

Unit 3 · Local features and robust fitting

3 lessons
  1. 03 Corner and Blob Detection Detect corners and blobs using gradients, the Harris structure tensor, NMS, and scale-space (LoG/DoG) responses. 15 min
  2. L3a Harris response & NMSSee how the structure tensor and response function highlight corners. 4 pts
  3. 04 Feature Detectors/Descriptors (SIFT) Describe SIFT from scale-space extrema to descriptors, and explain its invariances and basic matching. 16 min
  4. L4a SIFT descriptor & matching intuitionBuild gradient-histogram intuition and compare descriptor distances/ratio test. 4 pts
  5. 05 Model Fitting (Hough Transform, RANSAC) Fit lines/circles with Hough voting and estimate models robustly with RANSAC under outliers. 15 min
  6. L5a Hough votingMap edge points into parameter space and see votes accumulate for a line. 4 pts
  7. L5b RANSAC line fittingSample minimal sets, count inliers, and observe robust fitting amid outliers. 4 pts
  8. Checkpoint 3 — local features, descriptors, matching, and robust model fitting.

Unit 4 · Composition, alignment, and learning

3 lessons
  1. 06 Image Blending Combine images smoothly using alpha/feathering and multi-scale (pyramid/Laplacian) blending while handling seams and exposure differences. 14 min
  2. L6a Alpha, feathering & pyramidsCompare hard seams, feathered masks, and multi-band blending intuition. 4 pts
  3. 07 Image Registration Execute the end-to-end registration pipeline: match features, estimate transforms robustly, warp/resample, and evaluate alignment. 16 min
  4. L7a End-to-end registration pipelineStep from correspondences to an estimated transform and a warped overlay. 4 pts
  5. 08 Deep Learning–Based Computer Vision Explain CNN fundamentals and training basics, and relate learned representations to the classical CV tasks in this course. 17 min
  6. L8a Convolutional features & receptive fieldsVisualize how stacked convolutions build features and grow receptive fields. 4 pts
  7. L8b Classical pipelines to deep modelsCompare hand-crafted pipelines with end-to-end learned approaches on shared tasks. 4 pts
  8. Checkpoint 4 — blending, registration, and deep learning for vision.
