Lesson 06 — Image Blending

Alpha blending · feathering · seams/exposure · Gaussian/Laplacian pyramids · multi-band blending

Core ideas

  • Goal: combine overlapping images smoothly; seams arise from exposure/illumination differences and misalignment.
  • Alpha blending: I = αA + (1−α)B with mask α∈[0,1].
  • Hard mask: abrupt seam visible if photometry differs. Feathering: smooth transition reduces seam but can ghost misaligned content.
  • Gaussian pyramid: successive blur+downsample (multi-scale representation). Laplacian pyramid: band-pass details L_i = G_i − up(G_{i+1}); reconstruct by summing.
  • Multi-band/Laplacian blending: blend each band with Gaussian pyramid of mask → low frequencies transition broadly, high frequencies narrowly.

Key formulas

I(x,y)=α(x,y)A(x,y)+(1−α(x,y))B(x,y)
L_i = G_i - upsample(G_{i+1})
Blend: L_i = GM_i·LA_i + (1−GM_i)·LB_i

Choosing approach

  • Well-aligned, similar exposure → simple feather may suffice.
  • Exposure/color differences → multi-band blending hides seams better.
  • Misalignment/parallax → fix registration or choose seam; blending alone causes ghosts.

Pitfalls

  • Blending ≠ alignment (cannot fix parallax).
  • Too-narrow feather: seam remains; too-wide: blur/ghosting.
  • Need photometric normalization in some cases.
