  1. A 3×3 box blur kernel sums to 1. What happens to a flat region (all pixels equal to 100) after applying it (ignoring borders)?
    
    Answer
      
    It stays 100. A normalized averaging kernel preserves constant regions because the weighted sum of identical values equals that value.

  2. Why does a Sobel X kernel produce a strong response at a vertical edge but near zero in a flat region?
    
    Answer
      
    It computes a horizontal intensity difference (left vs right). A vertical edge has a large left–right change, while a flat region has nearly equal neighbors so positive and negative weights cancel.
