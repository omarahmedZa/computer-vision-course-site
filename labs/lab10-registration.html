Retrieval check

  1. Why is inverse warping used when producing the registered image?
    Answer

    It ensures every output (target-frame) pixel is assigned a value by sampling from the corresponding source location, avoiding holes that forward warping can create.

  2. If RANSAC finds few inliers among correspondences, what might that indicate?
    Answer

    Possible causes include many incorrect matches, an inappropriate motion model, insufficient overlap/texture, or strong non-rigid/parallax effects not explained by the chosen transform.
