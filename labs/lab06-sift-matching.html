Retrieval check


    
  
  1. Two candidate matches have distances 0.30 and 0.32. What does the ratio test suggest?
    
    Answer
    The ratio 0.30/0.32 ≈ 0.94 is high (close to 1), meaning the best match is not much better than the alternative — likely ambiguous, so it should be rejected.

  2. Why does L2-normalizing a descriptor help with illumination changes?
    
    Answer
    Uniform scaling of gradient magnitudes (e.g. contrast/brightness changes that scale responses) scales the descriptor vector; normalization removes that global scale so comparisons focus on the pattern/orientation distribution.
