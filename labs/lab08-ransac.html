Retrieval check

  1. In RANSAC, what makes a data point an “inlier” for a hypothesized model?
    Answer

    Its error (e.g. distance/residual) to that model is below a chosen threshold τ, meaning it is consistent with the hypothesis within tolerance.

  2. Why do we use a minimal sample (e.g. 2 points for a line) instead of many points to form each hypothesis?
    Answer

    A minimal sample maximizes the chance that it contains no outliers; using more points increases the likelihood of including an outlier and corrupting the hypothesis.
