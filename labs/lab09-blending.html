Retrieval check

  1. Why might a very wide feather be undesirable even if it hides the seam?
    Answer

    It blends over a large region, which can blur details, reduce contrast, or create ghosting/double exposure where structures don’t align perfectly.

  2. What is the key idea behind multi-band (pyramid) blending compared to single-scale feathering?
    Answer

    It blends different spatial frequency bands with different transition widths (broad for low frequencies, narrow for high frequencies), reducing seams in color/exposure while preserving fine detail.
