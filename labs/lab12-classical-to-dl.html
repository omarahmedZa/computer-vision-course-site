Retrieval check

  1. Give one situation where a classical geometric method (e.g. homography + RANSAC) is preferable or complementary to a purely learned approach.
    Answer

    When precise geometric consistency is required (e.g. planar alignment, camera calibration, mapping) or data is limited: estimating an explicit model with RANSAC enforces geometric constraints and can verify/regularize learned correspondences.

  2. What is a key limitation of relying only on end-to-end deep learning for vision tasks?
    Answer

    It depends heavily on representative labeled data and compute, can be sensitive to distribution shifts/adversarial cases, and may lack interpretability or explicit guarantees compared to model-based geometric methods.
