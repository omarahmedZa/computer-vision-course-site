Retrieval check

  1. In forward warping, why can holes appear in the output image?
    Answer

    Source pixels map to (often rounded) destination locations that may not cover every output pixel, especially under magnification/rotation, leaving some destinations unassigned.

  2. When might nearest-neighbor interpolation be preferred over bilinear?
    Answer

    When values represent discrete labels/masks or exact categories that must not be blended (e.g. segmentation maps), or when preserving original intensities/sharpness is more important than smoothness.
