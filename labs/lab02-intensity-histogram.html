Retrieval check

  1. If you add a constant β to every pixel and values hit 255, what happens to the histogram and to detail in highlights?
    Answer

    The histogram shifts right and piles up (saturates) at 255. Highlight detail is clipped/lost because distinct bright values become indistinguishable at the maximum.

  2. Why can two very different images have the same histogram?
    Answer

    A histogram counts intensity frequencies but discards spatial arrangement. Pixels can be rearranged into different structures while keeping the same distribution of values.
