Hough voting

You can map image edge points into (θ,ρ) parameter space and identify a line from accumulated votes.

Interactive demo

Points include inliers on a line plus outliers. Each point votes for all ρ = x cosθ + y sinθ across θ. The accumulator peak (brightest) corresponds to the dominant line.

Retrieval check

  1. Why is the normal form ρ = x cosθ + y sinθ preferred over y = mx + b for Hough line detection?
    Answer

    Because it can represent all lines including vertical ones with finite parameters, and it provides a bounded, well-behaved parameterization for the accumulator.

  2. If two different lines are present in the image, what would you expect to see in the accumulator?
    Answer

    Two distinct peaks (local maxima) at different (θ,ρ) cells, each receiving votes from points lying on its corresponding line.
